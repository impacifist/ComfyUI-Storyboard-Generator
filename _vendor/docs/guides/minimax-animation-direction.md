# H3 animation direction supplement

Application adaptation of the official MiniMax animation skill and its references listed in minimax-h3.md. This supplements direction generation, not the output schema or H3 renderer grammar.

Preserve the approved storyboard's visual style. Do not impose stylized 3D, a rendering engine, or a different animation medium. Use anticipation, expressive posing and follow-through only when they support the supplied action and style; never invent a new story event.

In context, establish the supplied location's useful landmarks, relative character placement and lighting. Track facing, depth, eyelines, relevant prop ownership and off-screen characters where established by the plan. Reference labels identify attachments, not new characters. Use supplied metadata only; do not claim to have inspected images or invent environment attachments.

Within each direction, describe a concise progression of preparation, action, speech, response and handoff. Avoid a rigid second-by-second checklist or timing every small gesture. Express progression in English prose without adding timestamp syntax or new JSON fields beyond the supplied output contract. The renderer supplies genuine shot-edit timestamps. Keep camera movement and expressions readable. Preserve canonical boundary states, continuing gestures and prop positions. Describe only source_offset through source_end when a source shot is split; never restart the whole action in the next clip.

Keep audio in the existing audio fields and deliveries, with clear ambience and action-related effects. Match audible events to the described action. Preserve assigned speaking intent; do not add spoken words. Avoid assigning lip movement to an off-screen voice. Keep music subordinate to dialogue and important effects, or use N/A when absent.

The approved text plan is authoritative. Do not instruct the video to display panel borders, sketch arrows or planning labels such as [char:...], [scene:...] or [hook:...]. Describe clean finished visuals; add subtitles or visible text only when the approved plan requests them. The application still supplies legitimate H3 shot, subject, picture and dialogue syntax.

Before returning, review identity, action readability, spatial continuity and audio intent against this segment. This is a direction review, not proof that a video was rendered. Preserve the application's English-only instructions, verbatim dialogue assembly and advisory-note policy. Do not change the clip plan, require the full story in every clip, or turn optional animation techniques into blocking warnings.

SCOPE: The brief describes the WHOLE original story. Its duration, shot_count, ending, required events and dialogue are global context, not obligations for each clip or a selected subset. The approved plan determines conversion scope. An intentionally omitted ending, beat or unselected dialogue is not a guide violation and must not produce a warning. Apply global story events only in their selected source shots. Never add omitted content just to satisfy the brief. Warn only about actual conflicts within the selected plan.

For official profiles, clip.duration is a whole-number generation duration of 4–15 seconds. When clip.content_duration is present, source shots occupy exactly that original interval; the remaining time is an explicit final-frame hold inserted by the renderer, not another shot or extra dialogue. Preserve every source timestamp, action, camera move and dialogue allocation. Do not stretch source actions to fill this hold or report the explicit padding as a timing conflict. Delay the final transition until after the hold. timeline_start/timeline_end still refer to source time, excluding padding.

For fixed time windows, source_offset/source_end specify the portion of the source shot to depict. Source duration and full actions provide context; never replay the whole shot in every window. Continue motion across windows, retaining scene transitions inside each clip. An intermediate segment has no fixed source start/end state: entry_boundary_id and exit_boundary_id replace those constraints. Original states inside boundary.original_start_context/original_end_context are context about the FULL source shot, not competing constraints on the intermediate boundary. Do not report a contradiction merely because those original endpoints differ from the interpolated boundary. Establish each shared boundary exactly once in the top-level boundaries array; the application inserts that same state at both adjoining clips. Direct both segments to approach/continue from that state.

The plan assigns dialogue once to a segment with sufficient estimated capacity when available, which may be a continuation segment. Use the assigned source.dialogue and dialogue_indices literally; do not move lines or repeat them. Speech-time estimates are a heuristic, not measured audio. Uncertain pacing, fast delivery, an estimated short interval or a resolved state transition belongs in notes, not blocking warnings. Never claim timing is verified. Do not shorten or accelerate dialogue to hide a timing concern. Only a demonstrated unsatisfied guide constraint inside the approved plan belongs in warnings. The explicit speech splitting mode may extend the story; the original brief duration remains global context, not a restriction on this authorized retimed plan.

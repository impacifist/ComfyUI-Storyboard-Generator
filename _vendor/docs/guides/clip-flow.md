# Conversational flow across generation segments

The plan is a continuous story divided into generation requests. A clip boundary is not automatically a dramatic ending, a pause, a new shot or a cue to start talking. Preserve the user's chosen clip lengths and all approved actions, original dialogue, speakers and shot order. Never invent events, omit words or accelerate speech to hide a capacity problem.

Return handoffs in the exact plan.handoffs order, each with its id, bridge and sound_bridge in English. The application inserts the SAME bridge on both sides. For continuous_shot, bridge describes the inherited action phase, direction, pace and continuing camera view; avoid a settled end pose or repeating the initial movement. For planned_cut, describe the approved edit and how the stimulus/response continues across it; do not force the same camera angle. For scene_change, preserve the approved change of place/time and sound; do not force uninterrupted movement or ambience. sound_bridge describes existing ambience, vocal rhythm or an intended audio transition; use N/A when there is no source-supported audio continuity. Do not include actual dialogue or rendering syntax in these fields.

Every shot has speech_cues, one short English cue for EACH assigned source dialogue line, in original line order. Use [] for a shot without dialogue. A cue states which approved gesture, perception or response starts that utterance. Use the current scene's facts rather than repeating a generic instruction. Speech can accompany an ongoing gesture when the source allows it; do not mechanically complete all gestures, wait, and only then speak. The next speaker responds to the prior stimulus rather than to the clip clock. Respect action prerequisites, intentional hesitation and pauses; do not invent an inhale or a delay at every beginning. Do not move a line to another shot or clip. The renderer inserts the exact original words separately.

Every shot also has pacing_weight, a finite number from 0 to 20. In auto mode only, it expresses the shot's relative need for ADDITIONAL performance time after the application reserves estimated speech and a small reading margin. Give physically involved actions, important anticipation and explicit intentional holds enough weight. Give a short nonverbal acknowledgment or connective glance little extra weight when a long pause would interrupt the conversation. Do not allocate a long silent tail to make an intermediate clip feel finished. Protect the dramatic intent of silent scenes; silence is not intrinsically an error. Use 0 when no extra time is useful. If all weights are 0, the application preserves the preview allocation. Other splitting modes preserve their existing shot times and ignore these weights.

In auto mode, preview shot times are an initial allocation, while clip IDs, clip lengths, source portions, original dialogue assignments and shot order remain fixed. The application applies the performance weights within that budget and assembles actual edit timestamps. Write qualitative action/speech/reaction progression, not exact per-second instructions or competing timestamps. Existing source durations are context, not a command to wait in a blank or motionless tail. If the speech estimate exceeds the budget, report an advisory note and preserve the approved material; do not claim the budget is satisfied, erase a real guide conflict or force a faster delivery.

H3 still uses the required shot headings and timestamps at genuine source-shot changes. The first visible shot in a continuation clip remains the same camera view when the source shot continues. Independent request packaging does not require a scene reintroduction. Finish only the final selected segment or an explicitly planned scene ending. Keep authored pauses, held 2D compositions and the intended final ending; do not add fades, silence, settling poses or an ending cadence merely because a generation request ends.

This policy changes text planning and prompt assembly. It does not change FPS, motion-context frames, trimming, video generation or the user's ComfyUI workflow, and it does not guarantee measured voice timing or seamless rendered video.
